Questions

Is it real wood?

It is a composite of PLA plastic and recycled wood fiber, so it prints with the look, texture and scent of wood. It is not solid timber.

Will it block RFID skimming?

Yes. The wallet is designed to block RFID signals from cards held inside it.

How long does an order take?

Each wallet is printed to order. We will confirm the lead time and shipping cost by email when we review your request.

How many cards does it hold?

Between 1 and 8 cards, plus folded cash under the elastic strap.
