Questions

Is it real wood?

It is a composite of PLA plastic and recycled wood fiber, so it prints with the look, texture and scent of wood. It is not solid timber.

How do I pay?

Checkout is through PayPal. You can pay with your PayPal balance or with a card, and PayPal collects your shipping address.

How long does an order take?

Each wallet is printed to order, so there is a short lead time before it ships.

How many cards does it hold?

Five cards, plus folded cash under the elastic strap.
