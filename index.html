Behind the Craft

We blend modern additive manufacturing with organic timber fibers to rethink everyday carry essentials.

Our Print Studio & Process

Every TimberVault wallet is calibrated, printed, and inspected in our boutique studio.

01. High-Precision Nozzles We print with hardened 0.6mm steel nozzles specially calibrated to allow dense wood fibers to flow without clogging or burning.
02. Hand Calibration Layer height and print temperatures are fine-tuned per batch to ensure deep natural grain patterns and zero warping.
03. Hand Finished After printing, each wallet undergo hand-sanding on key stress edges and sealed with a light organic satin finish.

Quality Guarantee

Because we print in small batches, every wallet undergoes a 5-point inspection before shipping.

  • Zero-slop card retention
  • Stress-tested spring tension
  • Smooth chamfered edges
  • Sustainably sourced bio-plastics
