About Wood Wallet

Two autistic entrepreneurs, one wood-fiber wallet, and a goal bigger than a sale.

Our story

Wood Wallet was started by MJ, who is from South Africa, and Aiden, who is from Canada. We are both autistic, and we are both entrepreneurs.

We are building Wood Wallet for two reasons. We are raising money for college, and we want to support autism research, a cause that is personal to us.

We design a wallet we would carry ourselves, then 3D print it in a PLA and wood-fiber composite so it has the grain and scent of real timber. Each one is printed to order.

20% goes to autism research

One fifth of every sale is donated to autism research. The rest helps pay for our college education.
