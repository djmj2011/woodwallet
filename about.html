About TimberVault

Wallets with the warmth of wood, made on a 3D printer.

TimberVault makes card wallets from a PLA and wood-fiber composite. The material gives each wallet the grain, texture and scent of timber, and printing lets us shape it precisely.

Every wallet is printed to order at 0.16 mm layer height with a 40% grid infill, so it stays rigid without feeling bulky. Up to 40% of the material is recycled wood fiber.
